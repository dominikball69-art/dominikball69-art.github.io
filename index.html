Split The Ball

Split The Ball is a YouTube channel of short 2D bouncing-ball physics simulations: last ball standing, ring escapes and overflow challenges, each with an outcome nobody can predict.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@SplitTheBall

The "Split The Ball uploader" is a private tool the channel owner uses to upload and schedule the channel's own videos and read the channel's own statistics. It is not offered to other users.
