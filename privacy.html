Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The Split The Ball uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Split The Ball YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to that channel, schedule them and read that channel's own video statistics.

Data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app accesses: the ability to upload videos to the owner's channel, the owner's channel and video information, and YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's videos (views, likes, watch time).

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to upload and schedule the channel's videos and to decide which kinds of videos to make next. The sign-in token and statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local files removes all stored data.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
